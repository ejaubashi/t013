Benno's Light — motion study view assets

These four JPEG files are interface-free browser captures of the project's authored gallery at commit fc18696. They are used as texture inputs for the separate Continuum and Lamina motion studies. They are not photographs or scans of a physical museum.

The paintings and archive imagery visible in these views retain the credits and provenance documented by the main project and docs/research/wall-art-collection.md. No images from external showcase templates were imported.
