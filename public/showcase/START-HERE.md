# Nocturne — showcase pack

Open **index.html** to browse the stills and play both films locally.

- **Landscape film:** `videos/nocturne-showcase-landscape.mp4` — 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, approximately 26 seconds. Suitable for a portfolio, a presentation, or a website feature.
- **Vertical reel:** `videos/nocturne-reel-vertical.mp4` — 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, approximately 17 seconds. Suitable for Reels, Stories, Shorts, and mobile previews.
- **Gallery stills:** eight high-quality JPEGs at 2560 × 1440. Two hero views also have PNG masters.
- **Social covers:** a 1080 × 1350 feed cover and a 1080 × 1920 story cover.
- **Captions:** three suggested posts in `captions.txt`.

The films use actual Nocturne gallery renders, slow eye-level camera movement, editorial titles, gentle dissolves, and the project's original procedural ambient sound. They are promotional edits of the experience. Play with sound or use them muted; the titles work both ways.

The stills and videos were captured from the Nocturne version of Benno's Light, commit f8807ab. The camera stays at a standing eye height of 1.72 m.

Experience: https://ejaubashi.github.io/t013/?view=spatial

Artwork reproduction credits and the source archive's CC0 license are in `credits/`. The gallery's historical photographic material retains the context and credits of the original Benno's Light project.
